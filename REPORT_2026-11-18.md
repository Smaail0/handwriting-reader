### Fixes and improvements made overnight

1. **Bug: constrained-decoding confidence could never exceed 0.5.** The best path was counted twice in the normalizer. Before the fix, every number field was sent to review; after it, they are calibrated as shown above. Fixed in `src/htr/decode.py`.
2. **Bug: two-digit years.** `15-02-98` became 2098. Dates now use a sliding century (up to 20 years ahead, otherwise the 1900s), and impossible dates such as 31/02 are rejected. Fixed in `src/htr/fields.py`.
3. **`text` fields are constrained to whole words.** A word must be all letters or all digits, which blocks misreads like `b4`, `0f` and `e1`. The gain is small (+0.4 points). The `free` type stays fully unconstrained, and codes like `A4` should use `code`.
4. **Model per field type.** `--model latin:text=other.onnx` routes one field type to a different recognizer. One use is a bigger model for prose with the 0.7 MB model for numbers.
5. **`propose_template.py`** finds field boxes on a crooked or scanned blank form. It recovered all 14 fields of the demo invoice.
6. The README now documents the forms pipeline, the training command for the field model, and all results.

# Forms and ruled paper (after user feedback on a job sheet photo)

**Feedback:** the browser demo was "not usable" on a photographed job sheet (printed labels, underlines, a parts table).

**Baseline, measured:** handwritten values had 51% CER and 4% word recall (`scripts/eval_photo.py`, truth in `samples/truth/jobsheet.txt`). The diagnosis was layout, not reading:
1. Fields from two columns were merged ("Address … Date …").
2. Underlines and table borders touched the letters, so strokes were glued to the grid, letters were cut ("pansion"), and two written lines merged.
3. The recognizer saw the underlines inside its crops.

**Built** (`page.py` and `web/src/page.js`, ported 1:1):
- **Ruled-line detection.** A straight horizontal run of at least 3 word heights (above a handwritten T crossbar), thin (at most max(5 px, 0.1 word height)), and joined across faint breaks only when at least 60% present. Vertical runs need 2.5 word heights, or 1.5 when both ends meet a horizontal rule (box and cell sides).
- **Removal.** Rules are removed from the ink, with stroke crossings kept, and whitened in the line crops.
- **Separators.** Rules separate fields: a vertical rule, or an underline starting or ending in the gap between two pieces of text. Grouping merges the closest pairs first, with the test applied between whole groups. Detector regions are cut at such boundaries when at least 2 text heights lie on each side.
- **Tall capitals.** Leftover tall capitals are attached to the line they sit beside.

**Decisions from measurements** (all on the job sheet unless noted):
- **Rules are not whitened in the detector's input.** It finds handwriting better with its writing lines in view: 18.5% vs 28.1%.
- **Rule lengths are relative to word height, not just page size.** At 5% of the page, the tall strokes of l, d and h became "column rules" and IAM error rose to 19.5%; with word-height lengths, 0 of 12 IAM pages are affected.
- **Wider crop padding was tested and did not help.** The remaining first-letter errors are the recognizer reading this writer's capitals.

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Job sheet value CER / word recall | 51% / 4% | **14.9% / 64%** |
| IAM pages, page CER (214) | 16.1% | **15.7%** |
| Synthetic Arabic pages | 5.6% | 5.5% |
| User photos, line count | 3 / 4 / 8 / 34 | unchanged |
| JS vs Python | | 20/20 pages same line count, 99.1% lines matched |

**Caveats:**
- This is one form photo, and the line-length thresholds were tuned while looking at it. It still needs more forms to confirm.
- The language model hurts codes ("M40 2AB" becomes "40 LAB"), so it is not for form fields.
- Not reviewed by the agents yet.
